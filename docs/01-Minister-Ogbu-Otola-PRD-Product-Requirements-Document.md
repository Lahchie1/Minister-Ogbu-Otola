# 1. Product Requirements Document (PRD)

**Product:** Minister Ogbu Otola Personal Website  
**Status:** Accepted and locked

---

**Product name and one-sentence idea:**  
Minister Ogbu Otola Personal Website — A focused digital hub that makes accurate Church of Christ teaching, published books, and curated research resources easily accessible so that Christians (especially youth to older adults in Nigeria) and serious seekers can find sound material without hunting through scattered Facebook posts and YouTube searches.

**Target users:**  
- Primary: Christians (youth through older adults) in Nigeria who want reliable Church of Christ doctrine, study material, and teaching from a known minister.  
- Secondary: People considering conversion or already studying the Church of Christ who need clear, uncompromised resources.  
- Tertiary (global vision): English-speaking Church of Christ members and Bible students outside Nigeria who value New Testament pattern teaching.

**Problem and current workaround:**  
Accurate COC research material and clear information about the Church of Christ are hard to find in one place. People currently rely on scattered Facebook posts, incomplete YouTube searches, and inconsistent online sources. This creates friction, incomplete understanding, and repeated questions directed at the minister.

**Goal and success measure:**  
Make sound COC teaching and the minister’s published work the easiest reliable option for the target users.

**Primary success measure (within 6 months of launch):**  
25% of site visitors click at least one external study link or submit a book inquiry.

**Secondary / diagnostic only:**  
Blog engagement metrics (once the blog has meaningful traffic).

**Core features:**  
- Books listing page: Display all published books with title, short description, cover image, and external purchase links (Amazon, sellers, etc.). Priority: High.  
- Curated external resources section: Organized list of high-quality research and study links useful for COC members and serious students. Priority: High.  
- About the Minister: Clear bio, vision, and contact path. Priority: High.  
- Blog / teaching archive: Space for articles, outlines, or key teachings. Priority: Medium-High (confirmed for v1).  
- Simple contact / inquiry form: Especially for book-related questions. Priority: Medium.

**Out of scope for version one:**  
- Selling books directly on the site (external links only).  
- Full church website functionality (service times, live streams, member portal, events calendar).  
- User accounts, comments, or community forums.  
- Mobile app.  
- Payment processing or e-commerce.

**User stories:**  
- As a young adult Christian in Nigeria, I want a single trustworthy page that lists the minister’s published books with clear purchase links, so I can obtain sound material without searching multiple platforms.  
- As a Bible student or preacher, I want a curated collection of external COC research links, so I can study efficiently instead of sifting through Facebook and random YouTube results.  
- As a seeker or new convert, I want a clear, uncompromised introduction to the minister and his teaching, so I can evaluate the Church of Christ message accurately.  
- As the minister, I want fewer repetitive questions about where to find sound study material, so my time can be spent on deeper teaching and personal ministry.

**Acceptance criteria:**  
- Given a visitor lands on the books page, when they view a book entry, then they see title, description, cover, and at least one working external purchase link.  
- Given a visitor is looking for study resources, when they open the resources section, then they find organized, clearly labeled external links that are relevant to COC research and study.  
- Given a visitor wants to contact the minister about a book, when they submit the inquiry form, then the message is delivered and a confirmation is shown.  
- Given the site is live for 90 days, when traffic and inquiry data are reviewed, then the primary success measure can be measured.
